## Round 2 Re-Verification of Core Design Sources

Per `TASK-2.md` Deliverable 2, all four core Round 1 design sources were re-opened directly from their primary URLs/PDFs on `2026-11-21`. Their full citations, publication and peer-review status, two-sentence methodological summaries, exact quoted numbers, and verbatim supporting quotations are documented in Section 6 of [recent-methods.md](file:///home/dege/chess/01-survey/recent-methods.md#L121-L195) and summarized below:

1. **Set-Based Retrograde Analysis (Stone, Sturtevant & Schaeffer, 2024/2025):** Re-opened `arXiv:2411.09089v1` (13 Nov 2024 preprint) and the subsequent peer-reviewed **IJCAI 2025** paper (`doi:10.24963/ijcai.2025/995`, pp. 8948–8955). All Round 1 numbers (`10^27` raw / `8×10^14` effective states, `1 week`, `50GiB`, `10^3` fewer search ops, `10^4` fewer DB entries, `39 trillion` Checkers $\le 10$-piece DB) are confirmed verbatim; updated row 35 above to include the peer-reviewed IJCAI 2025 28-card result (`10^30` -> `10^17` states, `1.4TiB`, `10^5` fewer sets).
2. **Capture-Quiet Decomposition (Pavlov, 9 Apr 2026):** Re-opened `arXiv:2604.07907v1` (non-peer-reviewed arXiv preprint). All Round 1 numbers (`517` endgames = `35` 3–4 pc / `42M` pos + `110` 5-pc + `372` 6-pc covering `6.5B` pos; capture fractions `8.5%` at 3-pc, `19.0%` at 4-pc, `25–30%` at 5-pc, `35–45%` at 6-pc, `50–70%` at 12-pc, `60–80%` at 20-pc; `~10^18` positions per 20-piece endgame) are confirmed verbatim. Clarified in row 38 that CQD's 517-endgame validation checks exact equality of violation counts (`v_total == full_v`) between decomposed CQD verification and full retrograde verification on CEGAR-trained decision-tree + residual models (including 121,188 violations on under-parameterized 4-piece `KBNvK` and 0 violations on all 110 5-piece and 372 6-piece endgames).
3. **Coq Tablebase Generator (Marzion, 21 Oct 2023):** Re-opened `https://emarzion.github.io/TB-Generator/` (non-peer-reviewed technical blog + Coq repo). Confirmed inductive `mate` / coinductive `draw` formalization, OCaml extraction for the Roman wheel Bear Game (3 hunters, 1 bear), and the explicit 4-piece feasibility limit.
4. **Mhalla and Prost 5×5 Proof (Mhalla & Prost, 2013):** Re-opened `arXiv:1307.7118v1` (26 Jul 2013 preprint) and verified its peer-reviewed publication in *ICGA Journal* 36(4):215–221 (Dec 2013). All Round 1 numbers (`9×10^18` positions, human-readable White and Black draw oracles, consumer-grade laptop compute with modified Stockfish) are confirmed verbatim; refined row 28 to note the peer-reviewed *ICGA Journal* venue and the exact wording `(up to +6)` for Stockfish's evaluation on drawn opening positions.
5. **Bonus Round 1 Upgrade — KRK Strategy in Isabelle/HOL + Z3 (Row 37):** Opened the full primary journal text of Janičić, Marić & Maliković (2019), *Logical Methods in Computer Science* 15(1):34:1–34:37 (`https://lmcs.episciences.org/5328/pdf`), upgrading row 37 from `UNVERIFIED` to `VERIFIED`.
